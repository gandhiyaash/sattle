Privacy Policy

Sattle · Effective 30 October 2026

Sattle is an app for splitting shared expenses with friends and settling up over the Bitcoin Lightning Network or UPI. It is run by the Sattle team ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Sattle app for Android, iOS and the web, the web app at sattle.tech (and at its earlier address, sattle.axiosiiitl.dev), pay links, and the Sattle server that they talk to.

  • We store what you enter to split expenses: group names, people's names, expenses, and payments between members.
  • We never hold your money. Payments go directly from one person's Lightning wallet or bank account to another's.
  • We don't ask for your email, phone number, contacts, location or photos.
  • We record how the app is used, such as which screens are opened and which buttons are pressed, to improve it. We don't use advertising or anyone else's analytics, we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell or rent data to anyone.
  • You can delete your account in the app, under Account, or ask us to delete your data at any time at reach.oms2004@gmail.com.

How the app is used

The app reports to our server what happens in it, so we can see how Sattle is used and improve it:

  • which screens, sheets and pages are shown, and which buttons are pressed, by their label (or a fixed name, where a label would contain someone's name);
  • when the app opens, goes to the background and comes back;
  • when it hands something to another app, and which kind: a wallet, a UPI app, the share sheet or the clipboard. The invoice, UPI ID, link or text itself is never included;
  • error messages shown on screen.

Each report carries a random ID the app creates for this installation, an ID for the current session, the platform (Android, iOS or web), the app version, and when it happened. When you are signed in, it is linked to your account, and what that installation reported before you signed up becomes part of your account's record. Pay link and group link tokens, invoices and UPI IDs are never recorded. On an iPhone the installation ID is kept in the keychain, so deleting and reinstalling the app on the same phone keeps it until the phone is erased.

Our server can also record each request the app makes (the kind of request, its result and how long it took, with the installation and session IDs) when we turn that record on to look into a problem.

Technical data

  • Server logs: when your device or browser contacts our server, our web server records the IP address, time, the address requested and the browser or app version. We use these logs only to keep the service running and secure. They are deleted after 14 days.
  • Network provider: our traffic passes through Cloudflare, which processes IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the service.

What we don't collect

Sattle does not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, or your device's advertising ID or other hardware identifiers. The only identifier it creates is the random installation ID described above. It contains no advertising software, and no analytics or crash-reporting software from anyone else. It never asks for your wallet's recovery phrase or private keys, or for card or bank details.

2. How we use data

We use the data above only to provide Sattle: to show groups and balances, record expenses, create and track payments and pay links, keep the service working and secure, and understand how the app is used so we can improve it. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or rent it.

3. Who can see it

  • Members of your groups see the group's name, its members, expenses, balances and payments.
  • Anyone you send a pay link to (or anyone they pass it on to) sees the payer's name, the payee's name, the group's name, the amount and the payment's status. A pay link does not show other members, other expenses or any other group data.
  • Anyone who has a group's link, if someone in the group has made one (or anyone they pass it on to), sees the group's name, every expense with its amount, who paid and each person's share, the members' names, and who owes whom. They can pay a debt but cannot change anything. In a group kept in rupees, when they choose to pay a debt by UPI they are shown the UPI ID of the person it is owed to, unless that person has turned this off. They can also ask to join the group, as one of its members who has not joined yet or under a new name; someone already in the group has to let them in, and from then on they see what its members see. A group has no such link until one of its members makes it, it keeps working until a member replaces it or turns it off, and any member can do either.
  • The person paying you sees the Lightning invoice, which includes the amount and a description containing your name and the group's name.
  • Someone who owes you, in a group kept in rupees, is shown your UPI ID if you have added one, so they can pay it. So is anyone paying you from that group's link, as described above; you can turn that off in the app, for all your groups or for one. A UPI ID often contains a phone number. Other members of the group see only that you take UPI, not the ID.

4. Service providers and third parties

  • Your Lightning wallet provider. If you connect a wallet, our server sends requests to it over Nostr Wallet Connect to create invoices and check their status. Each request contains the amount and a description with your name and the group's name. Requests travel through the Nostr relay named in your connection string; the contents are encrypted, but the relay can see when messages are sent. Your wallet provider handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • UPI apps and banks. If you pay or are paid over UPI, that payment is handled by the UPI apps and banks involved, under their own terms. The app opens the payer's UPI app with the payee's UPI ID, name, the amount and a note naming the group.
  • The Lightning Network. When a payment is made, it is routed by Lightning nodes that are not run by us. Like any Bitcoin payment, it is handled by the payer's and payee's wallets and the nodes along the route.
  • Nostr relays. A group's expenses, changes to them and confirmed payments are also published to public Nostr relays as a backup, encrypted with a key that only the group's members are given, so the group can be rebuilt without our server. Relays can't read the entries, but they can see our server's public key, a tag for each group, and when each entry was made. Relays are not run by us, and entries published to them can't be withdrawn. If you sign in with a remote Nostr signer, the sign-in request travels through the relay it names, encrypted.
  • Oracle Cloud hosts our server and database in India.
  • Cloudflare delivers and protects our website and server traffic.
  • CoinGecko provides the Bitcoin exchange rate. Our server requests the rate on its own; no user data is sent.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

5. Your wallet connection

The wallet connection string you paste is a secret that lets whoever holds it make requests to your wallet. We store it on our server and use it only to create and check invoices for payments made to you. We never use it to send money from your wallet. The app shows you which permissions the connection grants and warns you if it allows more than receiving. Disconnect, on the Wallet screen, makes our server forget it. You can also revoke the connection at any time in your wallet app, after which we can no longer use it.

6. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored on our server in India. All connections between the app and the server are encrypted with HTTPS. Access to the server is limited to the people who run Sattle. Data on the server is not end-to-end encrypted, so it can be read by us. No method of storing data is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep data

We keep groups, expenses and payment records for as long as the group exists, because they make up the group's shared history. Server logs are kept for 14 days. Wallet connections are kept until you disconnect them or ask us to remove them. The record of how the app is used is kept for a year for an account, and for 30 days for an installation that never made one; the server's request record, when on, for 30 days.

When you delete your account in the app, your sign-in key stops working, and your wallet connection, the pay links you made, your requests to join groups and your record of how the app was used are deleted. A group that only you could open is deleted with everything in it. In groups you share, your name and balance stay, because the other members' records need them. Encrypted backup entries already published to Nostr relays can't be withdrawn. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.

8. Your choices and rights

You can delete your account yourself in the app, under Account, and disconnect your wallet on the Wallet screen. You can also ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to remove your wallet connection, by emailing reach.oms2004@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU's GDPR, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. If someone added you to a group without your knowledge, you can ask us to remove your name.

9. Children

Sattle involves payments and is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
