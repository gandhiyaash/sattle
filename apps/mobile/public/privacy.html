Privacy Policy

Sattle · Effective 4 October 2026

Sattle is an app for splitting shared expenses with friends and settling up over the Bitcoin Lightning Network. It is run by the Sattle team ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Sattle app for Android, iOS and the web, the web app at sattle.axiosiiitl.dev, pay links, and the Sattle server that they talk to.

  • We store what you enter to split expenses: group names, people's names, expenses, and payments between members.
  • We never hold your money. Payments go directly from one person's Lightning wallet to another's.
  • We don't ask for your email, phone number, contacts, location or photos.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or rent data to anyone.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time at reach.oms2004@gmail.com.

Technical data

  • Server logs: when your device or browser contacts our server, our web server records the IP address, time, the address requested and the browser or app version. We use these logs only to keep the service running and secure. They are deleted after 14 days.

What we don't collect

Sattle does not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers or advertising ID. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software. It never asks for your wallet's recovery phrase or private keys, or for card or bank details.

2. How we use data

We use the data above only to provide Sattle: to show groups and balances, record expenses, create and track payments and pay links, and keep the service working and secure. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or rent it.

3. Who can see it

  • Members of your groups see the group's name, its members, expenses, balances and payments.
  • Anyone you send a pay link to (or anyone they pass it on to) sees the payer's name, the payee's name, the group's name, the amount and the payment's status. A pay link does not show other members, other expenses or any other group data.
  • Anyone who has a group's link, if someone in the group has made one (or anyone they pass it on to), sees the group's name, every expense with its amount, who paid and each person's share, the members' names, and who owes whom. They can pay a debt but cannot change anything. A group has no such link until one of its members makes it, and any member can replace it or turn it off.
  • Anyone who has a group's invite, if someone in the group has made one (or anyone they pass it on to), sees the group's name, the name of the member who made the invite, and the names of the members who have not joined yet. They can join the group as one of those members, and from then on they see what its members see. An invite stops working a week after it is made, and any member can replace it or turn it off.
  • The person paying you sees the Lightning invoice, which includes the amount and a description containing your name and the group's name.

4. Service providers and third parties

  • Your Lightning wallet provider. If you connect a wallet, our server sends requests to it over Nostr Wallet Connect to create invoices and check their status. Each request contains the amount and a description with your name and the group's name. Requests travel through the Nostr relay named in your connection string; the contents are encrypted, but the relay can see when messages are sent. Your wallet provider handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • The Lightning Network. When a payment is made, it is routed by Lightning nodes that are not run by us. Like any Bitcoin payment, it is handled by the payer's and payee's wallets and the nodes along the route.
  • Oracle Cloud hosts our server and database in India.
  • Cloudflare delivers and protects our website and server traffic.
  • CoinGecko provides the Bitcoin exchange rate. Our server requests the rate on its own; no user data is sent.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

5. Your wallet connection

The wallet connection string you paste is a secret that lets whoever holds it make requests to your wallet. We store it on our server and use it only to create and check invoices for payments made to you. We never use it to send money from your wallet. The app shows you which permissions the connection grants and warns you if it allows more than receiving. You can revoke the connection at any time in your wallet app, after which we can no longer use it.

6. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored on our server in India. All connections between the app and the server are encrypted with HTTPS. Access to the server is limited to the people who run Sattle. Data on the server is not end-to-end encrypted, so it can be read by us. No method of storing data is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep data

We keep groups, expenses and payment records for as long as the group exists, because they make up the group's shared history. Server logs are kept for 14 days. Wallet connections are kept until you ask us to remove them. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to remove your wallet connection, by emailing reach.oms2004@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU's GDPR, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. If someone added you to a group without your knowledge, you can ask us to remove your name.

9. Children

Sattle involves payments and is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.

11. Contact
