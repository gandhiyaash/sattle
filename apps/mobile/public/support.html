Support

Sattle

Sattle splits shared expenses with friends and settles them over the Bitcoin Lightning Network or UPI. Only one person in a group needs the app; everyone else can use the group's link in a browser.

Write to reach.oms2004@gmail.com with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Found a bug? You can also open an issue on GitHub.

The app answers most questions itself: open Wallet, then FAQ.

A payment went wrong

Sattle never holds money. A Lightning payment goes from the payer's wallet straight to the wallet of the person owed, and a UPI payment from one bank account to another. If money left your wallet or bank and didn't arrive, the wallet or bank that sent it can trace it. Don't pay the same debt twice: the person you paid can mark it settled in the group.

A new phone, or a lost one

Your account is a name and a sign-in key. On the old device, Account → Copy sign-in key gives you the key; on the new one, I have a sign-in key on the welcome screen signs you in with all your groups. If you linked a Nostr key, Sign in with Nostr works too. If a phone that was signed in is lost, replace the key under Account from another device, and the lost phone is signed out.

Deleting your account

Open Account → Delete account in the app. What is deleted and what stays is set out in the privacy policy. You can also ask us by email.
